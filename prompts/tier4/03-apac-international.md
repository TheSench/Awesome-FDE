### Chinese Role Title Breakdown

The title "Forward Deployed Engineer" is almost never used in APAC postings. Here's what each equivalent title typically owns, and which maps most closely to FDE work:

| Chinese Title | English | What it actually involves | FDE proximity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI 实施工程师** | AI Implementation Engineer | End-to-end deployment of AI systems into customer environments; writes integration code; owns delivery outcomes | **Closest to FDE** — this is the one to target |
| **解决方案工程师** | Solutions Engineer | At large companies: pre-sales/post-sales demos and POCs. At AI startups: often owns production code and customer delivery — effectively FDE | **Strong overlap** at startups; weaker at large enterprises |
| **技术交付工程师** | Technical Delivery Engineer | Project/delivery focused; closer to technical PM; often writes integration code for customer handoff | **Moderate overlap** — less independent customer ownership |
| **AI 解决方案架构师** | AI Solutions Architect | Senior, design-oriented; less hands-on coding than FDE; more advisory | **Senior path** — where FDEs grow into, not where they start |
| **客户成功工程师** | Customer Success Engineer | Post-sale and relationship-focused; lighter on production coding than FDE | **Weak overlap** — good for context but not the target |
| **模型部署工程师** | Model Deployment Engineer | MLOps-adjacent; focused on serving infrastructure, not customer-facing deployment | **Technical niche** within the FDE skill set |

**Search terms to use on Chinese job boards:**
- "AI 实施" — will surface implementation-focused roles
- "AI 解决方案" — broader; includes architect and sales roles
- "技术交付 AI" — delivery-focused roles at larger companies
- "LLM 部署" — more technical/infra-leaning roles

### Why Crypto Engineers Fit APAC Specifically

The README explains the general crypto → FDE translation. Here's what makes crypto engineers particularly well-positioned for *APAC* FDE roles specifically:

**1. The largest crypto companies are APAC-headquartered.** OKX (Seychelles-registered, HK/Shenzhen operations), Binance, HashKey, and Bybit are all APAC-native. They're not "entering" APAC — they *are* APAC. An engineer who understands how these companies work, what their infrastructure looks like, and how they interact with enterprise customers is already ahead of a US SWE parachuting in.

**2. Crypto infrastructure maps to what APAC fintech needs from AI FDEs.** HK and Singapore's fintech ecosystems (MAS sandbox in Singapore, HKMA open API initiatives in HK) are converging crypto-native infrastructure with traditional financial services. Companies need engineers who understand both WebSocket-based real-time feeds, high-throughput event processing, and how to deploy AI on top of them. That's a crypto engineer's daily work.

**3. Data sovereignty is a constant constraint in APAC, and crypto engineers understand it.** China's data localization laws (PIPL, DSL), Singapore's PDPA, and financial regulators across APAC require that customer data stays within defined geographic boundaries. Crypto engineers are used to operating with strict data residency constraints — not fighting them. This is a genuine differentiator when an APAC enterprise customer asks "can your system run fully on-prem?"

**4. The APAC crypto community is a network, not just a background.** HK and Singapore's crypto ecosystems are relationship-dense. Engineers who've worked at or with APAC crypto companies have often already built trust with the people who are now building enterprise AI at those same companies or at adjacent firms. ### Customer Relationship Dynamics in APAC

**Trust-building (guanxi) matters more than in Western enterprise contexts.** Decisions about technology vendors in Chinese enterprises are rarely made purely on technical merit — long-term relationship and track record are often more important. An FDE who parachutes in for a 2-week POC and leaves hasn't built enough relationship equity to win follow-on work, even if the POC succeeded technically.

**POC culture is active.** Customers in APAC (particularly in China) expect detailed proofs of concept before committing to a contract. This isn't unusual skepticism — it's how enterprise software is sold. FDEs need to treat POC delivery with the same quality bar as production, because it's being evaluated as if it were production.

**Technical authority is established through demonstrated knowledge in senior technical meetings.** In Western FDE contexts, authority often comes from delivery track record. In APAC enterprise contexts, especially in China, demonstrating technical depth directly to senior technical decision-makers (CTO, CIO, Head of AI) early in the engagement matters disproportionately. The relationship is established at the top-of-house level, then handed down.

**Hierarchy in decision-making is real.** The end users of the system rarely make the final purchasing decision. An FDE who wins over the analyst team but loses the IT director loses the deal. Mapping the decision hierarchy explicitly at the start of an engagement is a survival skill in APAC that's optional in many Western contexts.

### Where to Find APAC FDE-Equivalent Roles

**China:**
- **BOSS直聘 (BOSS Zhipin)** — primary job board; use the search terms listed above
- **拉勾网 (Lagou)** — tech-focused; skews toward product and engineering roles at AI companies
- **智联招聘 (Zhilian)** — broader professional hiring; useful for large enterprise roles (Ant Group, Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud)

**Hong Kong / Singapore:**
- **LinkedIn** — works well for HK and SG; search "AI Implementation", "Solutions Engineer AI", "Forward Deployed"
- **OKX Careers** — search "Solutions Engineer" or "AI Engineer"; roles are in HK and Seychelles/remote
- **HashKey Group** — watch their careers page directly; roles under "Business Development + Engineering" overlap

**What these roles look like in practice:**
- OKX is building internal AI tooling *and* enterprise products for institutional clients. An FDE-equivalent there might own deploying an AI-powered compliance monitoring tool for a bank that uses OKX's institutional infrastructure.
- Ant Group (Alibaba Financial Services) runs an AI division (Ant Intelligence) that's deploying AI into financial services across APAC. The "AI 实施工程师" roles here are large-scale enterprise deployments with significant engineering ownership.
- Tencent Cloud and Alibaba Cloud both have partner/ISV programs where FDE-equivalent engineers support enterprise customers deploying on their AI services — closer to a cloud solution architect but with production coding expectations.
