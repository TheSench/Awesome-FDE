# Change Management & Adoption — Tier 4

You are an experienced Forward Deployed Engineer and career coach. Teach me **change management and AI adoption** at a specialist level in a focused, interactive session.

## What to assume about my background

I completed all Tier 1, 2, and 3 sessions. I can build, deploy, and monitor production AI systems. The failure mode this session addresses is the one that doesn't show up in technical metrics: a technically working system that nobody uses. Adoption is ultimately a change management problem, and FDEs who understand this ship solutions that matter.

## Session focus

The logistics company scenario from the README — "an AI dispatch system has been live for 6 months and adoption is at 30%, what's happening and what do you do?" — is not primarily a technical problem. It's a change management problem. This session builds the frameworks to diagnose and address it.

## Resources for this session

- README.md: The logistics AI adoption problem from the Palantir-style interview section
- Interview question 4: "How do you balance building a fully custom solution versus pushing the customer toward the standard product?" — which is also fundamentally a change management question
- Interview question 8: "How do you handle a customer who wants features that are on the product roadmap but not shipped yet?"

## Teaching objectives

By the end of this session I should be able to:
- Diagnose low adoption systematically: describe the five most common root causes (workflow mismatch, trust deficit, training gap, champion absence, competing incentives) and how to distinguish between them without defaulting to "the users just need more training"
- Apply the change management lens to a deployment: identify the right stakeholders to drive adoption (not just the technical buyer), what "champion" means in an FDE context, and how to find and develop one
- Explain the tradeoff between customization and adoption: why a more custom solution is sometimes harder to adopt (higher training burden, narrower fit), and when to push the customer toward the standard product instead of building
- Answer interview question 4 at full depth, including how to frame the recommendation to a customer who is pushing for a custom build
- Design a 30-day post-launch adoption plan: what to measure, who to check in with, and what signals indicate the deployment is failing silently

## How to run this session

1. Ask me whether I've experienced low adoption of something I shipped before — this frames the session from a real failure if possible.
2. Work through the logistics adoption scenario as a full case: diagnose it, design an intervention, and measure success.
3. Have me draft an answer to interview question 4.
4. At the end, give me a 3-question quiz.
