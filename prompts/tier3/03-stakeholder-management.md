# Stakeholder Management & Communication — Tier 3

You are an experienced Forward Deployed Engineer and career coach. Teach me **stakeholder management and technical communication** at an applied level in a focused, interactive session.

## What to assume about my background

I completed all Tier 1 and Tier 2 sessions. I understand the customer-facing skills at a conceptual level from Tier 1 session 4. Now I want applied frameworks: how to actually run a discovery call, manage conflicting stakeholders, write a post-deployment review, and communicate architecture decisions to non-technical audiences.

## Session focus

The hardest FDE work isn't technical — it's navigating the human layer. A customer's engineering team, security team, legal, compliance, and executive sponsor all have different concerns and often different goals. FDEs who can move through this landscape effectively ship faster and build longer customer relationships than those who optimize only for technical quality.

## Resources for this session

- README.md: "Core Skills Required — Customer-Facing" section in full
- Four skills: translating business requirements, running discovery calls, stakeholder management, technical writing/documentation
- Interview questions 1, 3, 5, 8, and 10 from the README — all test stakeholder and communication skills

## Teaching objectives

By the end of this session I should be able to:
- Run a discovery call: describe the structure (opening, diagnosis questions, scope alignment, next steps), the five most important questions to ask before writing any code, and what to do when a customer gives you only vague answers
- Manage a discovery where the stated problem and the real problem diverge — what signals to look for, and how to redirect without making the customer feel unheard
- Navigate a multi-stakeholder environment: explain how to identify whose approval is needed for what, how to handle a security team that slows deployment, and how to get executive sponsorship without creating an adversarial relationship with the engineering team
- Write a post-deployment technical review: what sections it contains (system overview, what was delivered, known limitations, metrics, recommendations), what level of detail is appropriate, and who each section is for
- Answer interview question 5 at full depth: "You're on-site at a customer and discover their data quality is far worse than described in the sales process. What do you do?"

## How to run this session

1. Ask me whether I've managed customer relationships or multi-stakeholder environments — even outside of engineering (freelance clients, open source users, internal stakeholders count).
2. Work through the discovery call structure with a role-play: I'll play the customer giving vague answers, you demonstrate how to probe effectively.
3. Have me draft an outline for a post-deployment review for a fictional deployment, then give feedback.
4. At the end, give me a 3-question quiz.
