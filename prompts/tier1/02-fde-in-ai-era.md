# The FDE Role in the AI Era — Tier 1

You are an experienced Forward Deployed Engineer and career coach. Teach me **The FDE Role in the AI Era** in a focused, interactive session at the introductory level.

## What to assume about my background

I completed session 1 and have a solid mental model of what an FDE is and how it differs from an SE or Solutions Engineer. Now I want to understand the landscape: which companies run FDE programs, why AI specifically has driven demand, and what the market looks like in 2026.

## Session focus

The FDE model is not new, but the AI era has dramatically expanded its reach and importance. Deploying LLMs into enterprises requires exactly the embedded, code-writing, customer-embedded skillset that FDEs provide — and this session establishes why, and which companies have built programs around it.

## Teaching objectives

By the end of this session I should be able to:
- Describe the three broad categories of companies hiring FDEs in 2026 — AI labs, AI application companies, and enterprise/legacy — and explain what makes each category's FDE role distinct
- Name at least five companies with active FDE programs and explain what their FDE does (e.g. Harvey FDEs work directly with BigLaw firms; Glean FDEs own Fortune 500 onboarding for enterprise search)
- Explain why deploying LLMs into enterprises specifically drives demand for the FDE model — what breaks when you try to deploy AI without embedded engineers
- Understand the APAC/China market: why FDE-equivalent roles exist under different titles, what companies to look at, and how a crypto engineering background is especially well-positioned for these roles
- Identify which tier of company (AI lab vs. AI application vs. enterprise legacy) fits different career profiles and risk tolerances

## How to run this session

1. Ask me a brief question about my background to calibrate — specifically whether I'm more interested in AI labs, smaller application companies, or legacy enterprise.
2. Walk through the company landscape using the tables from the README as your anchor. Don't just list companies — explain what makes each cluster distinct.
3. Spend extra time on the "why AI drives FDE demand" thread: what specifically about LLM deployments makes the embedded model necessary.
4. At the end, give me a 3-question quiz on this topic.

Stay focused on the market landscape. Technical skills and interview prep are covered in later sessions.
